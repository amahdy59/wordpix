# UNIT 3

## Is Online Shopping Better Than Shopping in Stores?

**Level:** B1  
**Topic:** Consumer Life & Technology  
**Speaking Skill:** Discussing advantages and disadvantages

## 1. Big Question & Warm-up

### Big Question

**Is Online Shopping Better Than Shopping in Stores?**

Discuss:

1. What was the last thing you bought online?
2. What do you prefer to buy in a physical store?
3. What can go wrong when you shop online?

### Quick Vote

**A.** Online shopping is better.  
**B.** Shopping in stores is better.  
**C.** I use both equally.

## 2. Read & Understand

### Click or Walk Into the Store?

Online shopping has changed everyday life. Instead of travelling to several stores, people can compare hundreds of products from a sofa, order at any time, and have purchases delivered to their door. For many customers, this is extremely convenient.

Online marketplaces can also offer a wider selection than a local shop. It is easy to shop around, compare prices, read reviews, and search for a product that may not be available nearby. OECD research notes that digital markets can give consumers greater choice and convenience.

However, convenience does not remove every problem. A product can look different in a photograph, the size may be wrong, delivery can be late, and some reviews can be misleading. Customers may need to send back products they do not want. Fraud and unsafe products are also concerns in some online marketplaces.

Physical stores offer a different experience. Customers can touch a product, try on clothes, ask an employee a question, and take the item home immediately. Shopping in person can also be social. On the other hand, stores have opening hours, limited stock, and sometimes higher prices.

The best choice may depend on the product. Buying a familiar book online is very different from buying shoes that need to fit perfectly. A low price can look attractive, but if a product is difficult to return or is poor quality, it may not be good value after all.

Online and offline shopping are not simply competitors. Many people now move between both, using the internet to research and a store to test—or doing exactly the opposite.

### In Short

Online shopping is **convenient**, offers a wide **selection**, and makes it easy to **shop around**. But customers may face **misleading** information, delivery problems, or need to **send back** products that are not **good value**.

## 4. Conversation Toolkit

### Talking About Advantages and Disadvantages

**The main advantage is...**

> The main advantage is that you can compare prices quickly.

**The downside is...**

> The downside is that you cannot try the product first.

**Another benefit is...**

> Another benefit is home delivery.

**The problem with that is...**

> The problem with that is that returns can take time.

## 5. Quick Quiz

**1. “Selection” means:**  
A. range of choices B. delivery time C. final price D. store employee

**2. If you “shop around,” you:**  
A. walk around one shop B. compare options before buying C. return everything D. buy immediately

**3. If a product “sells out,” it:**  
A. becomes cheaper B. is returned C. has no stock left D. is damaged

**4. “Too good to be true” suggests that an offer may be:**  
A. ordinary B. suspicious C. slow D. local

**5. What benefit of online shopping does the reading mention?**  
A. You can always try products first. B. It can offer wider choice. C. Delivery is always free. D. Reviews are always accurate.

**6. What is one risk mentioned?**  
A. Stores never open. B. Products cannot be photographed. C. Reviews can be misleading. D. People cannot compare prices.

**7. Why might a physical store be better for shoes?**  
A. Shoes never sell online. B. You can try the fit. C. Stores always have lower prices. D. Delivery is impossible.

**8. Why can a cheap product be poor value?**  
A. A low price always means low quality. B. It may be poor quality or difficult to return. C. Cheap products cannot be delivered. D. Customers prefer expensive things.

**9. What is the main idea?**  
A. Online shopping is always best. B. Stores should disappear. C. Each method has advantages depending on the product and situation. D. People should stop reading reviews.

**10. Which is correct?**  
A. I sent back the shoes. B. I sent the shoes back on. C. I sent back on the shoes. D. I send backed the shoes.

## 6. Discussion

### 1. Your Habits

What do you buy online most often, and why?

### 2. Trust

How much do you trust online reviews?

### 3. Price

Would you buy from an unknown website if the price were 40% lower?

### 4. Experience

Which products should always be seen or tested in person?

### 5. Future

Could physical stores ever disappear in your city?

### 6. The Big Question

What matters more when shopping: maximum convenience or maximum confidence in what you are buying?

## 7. Speaking Challenge

### Where Should We Buy It?

You have to buy five things: **running shoes, a phone charger, a birthday gift, a sofa, and groceries for tonight**. For each item, decide whether to buy online or in a store. Explain the advantage, possible problem, and what would change your decision.

### Useful Language

> The main advantage is...

> The downside is...

> I would shop around first.

> For this product, I would rather...
