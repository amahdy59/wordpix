## Progression at a Glance

### B1 — EXPRESS — Units 01–10

Learners express opinions, give reasons and examples, compare alternatives, agree/disagree politely, and make simple joint decisions.

### B2 — DEFEND — Units 11–20

Learners support claims, respond to objections, speculate, make concessions, discuss consequences, persuade, and summarise competing arguments.

### C1 — ANALYSE — Units 21–30

Learners identify assumptions, qualify claims, evaluate evidence, reframe questions, balance competing interests, and synthesise multiple viewpoints.

### C2 — NAVIGATE — Units 31–40

Learners question premises, define contested ideas, reason under uncertainty, analyse second-order effects, distinguish closely related concepts, and build nuanced syntheses.
