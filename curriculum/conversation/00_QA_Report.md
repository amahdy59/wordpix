The progression is intentionally driven by **cognitive and interactional complexity as well as length**. B1 readings remain concrete; B2 adds counterargument and evidence; C1 increases abstraction and multi-perspective analysis; C2 focuses on conceptual boundaries, uncertainty, premises, and second-order effects.

## Scope of QA

The checks above combine automated structural validation with editorial review. They substantially reduce formatting, duplication, omission, and progression errors; they should not be interpreted as a guarantee that future research updates will never require revision. Time-sensitive factual claims should be rechecked when the curriculum is republished or materially updated.
