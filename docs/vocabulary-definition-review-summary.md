## Review rules

- Confirm the pictured, unit-specific sense before approval.
- Write original copy; do not paste Oxford, Cambridge, WordNet, or other dictionary wording into the product.
- Keep A1-A2 definitions at 8-18 words and B1-B2 definitions at 10-24 words.
- Treat current C1 card labels as B2-definition targets unless the curriculum level is separately corrected.
- Clear every automated flag and set `approved` to `yes` only after human review.
