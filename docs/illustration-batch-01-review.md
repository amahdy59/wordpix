triangular-face tetrahedron; unambiguous front/behind scene; double selection borders
on selected number/math displays. Exact counts and positions checked against contexts.

the exact encoded total. Every WebP decoded identically to its PNG. Dimensions 1200 × 675.

still match; unchanged or unreviewed media remains as authored.

The images supplement the exact usage scenes. They do not replace unrelated
word-specific sentences (such as two bags with a folders illustration).
This is an image-specific review, not a full WCAG conformance audit or a claim
that all lessons have been approved for release. Existing lesson approval gates remain.
