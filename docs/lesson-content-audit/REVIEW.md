## Editorial evidence and method

`editorial-corrections.json` records each changed field with unit ID, content ID and before/after text. `editorial-summary.json` holds the latest counts. Counts are corrected fields, not distinct words. Repair batches retain individually authored replacements.

The legacy `docs/vocabulary-definition-review.csv` is reconciled for definitions actually corrected in the ledger. Image confirmation and publication approval remain separate. Untouched source drafts remain a review queue; they are not approved by a structural audit. Its historical summary is not the count of unresolved runtime definitions.

Review uses the lesson-specific meaning and part of speech, concise original learner definitions, natural Modern Standard Arabic, consistent terminology and distinct meanings for related words. It avoids unsupported health claims and preserves canonical religious source text.

Specialist checks used primary references: [OpenStax geometry](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/c-geometric-formulas), [NIST geometry](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/circumference-area-and-volume), [MedlinePlus nebulizers](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000006.htm), [MedlinePlus ventilators](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000458.htm), [NASA solar-system facts](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/solar-system-facts/), [U.S. Courts glossary](https://www.uscourts.gov/glossary), [NEI diabetic retinopathy](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/diabetic-retinopathy), [NIAMS calcium and vitamin D](https://www.niams.nih.gov/health-topics/calcium-and-vitamin-d-important-bone-health), [EPA dental effluent](https://www.epa.gov/eg/dental-effluent-guidelines), and [Investor.gov P/E ratio](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/price-earnings-ratio).
