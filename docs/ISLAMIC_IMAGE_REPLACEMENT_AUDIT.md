# Islamic-tradition image replacement audit

Audit rule requested by the product owner:

1. Replace an image when an adult woman is visibly shown without a hijab/head covering.
2. Replace an image when a woman is presented in revealing, tight, intimate, swim, dance, spa, or otherwise immodest clothing/context.

This is a visual-content standard, not an inference about any person's religion. Children, mannequins, isolated garments, hands, and images where a person's gender cannot be seen clearly are excluded from the definite list.

## Figma regeneration direction

For replacements, preserve the vocabulary meaning while showing adult women in a clear, naturally draped hijab and loose, opaque, long-sleeved clothing. Avoid exposed hair/neck, tight silhouettes, short sleeves/skirts, cleavage, transparent fabric, swimwear, intimate touch, massage-table exposure, and mixed dancing. Keep the result photorealistic, culturally respectful, uncluttered, centered, and readable at card-thumbnail size.
