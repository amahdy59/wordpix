# WordPix vocabulary definition standard

## Purpose

Every vocabulary image needs a short meaning that identifies the intended sense and helps a learner understand the word without relying on the image. The same text may also support accessible assessment image descriptions and future narration, so placeholder or circular copy is not acceptable.

## Editorial rules

- Write original WordPix copy. Oxford and Cambridge may be consulted to confirm sense, usage, level and regional variation; do not copy their definitions or examples unless WordPix has an applicable licence.
- Define the sense taught by the unit and pictured by the image. Add another sense only when the lesson teaches it.
- Use one sentence ending with a period.
- Prefer 8–18 words for A1–A2 and 10–24 words for B1–B2.
- Use familiar words at or below the headword's target CEFR level.
- Avoid the headword itself, circular definitions, encyclopedic detail, idioms and unexplained specialist terms.
- Include `UK` or `US` wording only when the distinction helps the learner.
- Describe verbs with an infinitive phrase and nouns with a concrete category plus their purpose or distinguishing feature.
- Review the definition beside its image, Arabic gloss, part of speech and examples before approving it for audio.

## Examples

| Word       | WordPix learner definition                                              |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Toilet     | A bathroom fixture used for getting rid of waste from the body.         |
| Sink       | A bowl with taps and a drain, used for washing your hands or face.      |
| Shower     | A place or device that sprays water over you so you can wash your body. |
| Wash hands | To clean your hands with soap and water.                                |

## Rollout and validation

1. Complete one unit at a time, beginning with Level 1 and the units learners reach most often.
2. Check ambiguous labels against the image and lesson materials before choosing a sense.
3. Run the vocabulary integrity tests. They validate completed definitions and prevent the recorded placeholder debt from increasing.
4. Review the unit in Bilingual and Immersion modes, at 200% zoom, and in assessment alt text.
5. Approve text before generating audio. Text changes after recording create replacement audio cost.

The current placeholder allowance is migration debt, not acceptable content. Reduce its pinned ceiling whenever a unit is completed; never raise it for a new import.
