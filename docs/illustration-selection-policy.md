# Illustration selection policy

User instructions govern selection. Preserve every source image and video in
Figma, R2 and local storage, including rejected and superseded assets.

Prefer object-focused illustrations, diagrams and scenes without people when
these clearly teach the concept or phrase. Do not add women unnecessarily,
especially unveiled women. When a person is essential, review the exact learning
context and representation before approving the asset. Do not substitute a
different person or gender when that would contradict the question text.

An image must clearly support the current question's objects, quantities,
colors, positions and actions. Reject incorrect, ambiguous, answer-labelled or
poorly framed images. Use an explicitly labelled placeholder with sufficient
textual evidence until a suitable replacement is reviewed. Record a stable
question ID, source reference, rejection reason and replacement brief.

The existing café trial video and poster remain preserved locally and in R2.
They are withheld from learner selection because they feature unveiled women
in a counting situation that does not require women. Replace them with an
object-focused counter, tray and cups scene before approving the trial.

The 76 reused images and 50 vector illustrations previously integrated are
object-focused. Future batches must apply this policy before upload and linking.
